  • — Flakiness or rough texture
  • — Dull-looking skin
  • — Fine dehydration lines
02 WHAT MAY CONTRIBUTE

More than one factor can influence how a skin concern appears or behaves.

  • — Low moisture levels
  • — Moisture loss
  • — Over-cleansing
  • — Barrier stress
03 WHAT YOUR SKIN MAY NEED

  • — Daily protection

WHAT TO BE CAREFUL WITH

Don't let your routine
work against you.

More products do not always mean better results. A routine should support your skin rather than overwhelm it.

01

Over-exfoliation

Excessive exfoliation can add unnecessary stress to the skin.

02

Too many active ingredients

Layering multiple strong treatments can make a routine harder to balance.

03

Ignoring your skin barrier

Targeting a concern while ignoring overall skin comfort can create an unbalanced routine.
