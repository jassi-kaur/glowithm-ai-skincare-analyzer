BRIGHTENING · BARRIER SUPPORT

Niacinamide

Vitamin B3

Water-soluble • Beginner-friendly • Daily use
WHAT IT IS

Niacinamide is a form of vitamin B3 commonly used in skincare to support the skin barrier, balance excess oil and improve the appearance of uneven skin tone.

WHAT IT DOES
  • Supports the skin barrier
  • Helps control excess oil
  • Improves the appearance of uneven skin tone
HOW IT WORKS

Niacinamide supports several functions of the skin barrier and can help regulate excess sebum while supporting a more even-looking complexion.

BEST FOR
Acne-prone skin Uneven skin tone Visible dark spots Weak skin barrier
SKIN TYPES
Oily Combination Normal Dry Sensitive
HOW TO USE

Can generally be used once or twice daily after cleansing and before moisturizer.
